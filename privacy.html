Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

LunaTheBot is a personal experimental project. This policy explains, at a high level, how information may be handled when the project is used.

Information used

The project may process information needed to authenticate with TikTok and publish content, such as account authorization information, media, captions, and other publication details supplied for a post.

How information is used

Information is used only as needed to operate the project, authenticate the associated TikTok account, and create or publish the requested content.

Third-party services

TikTok and Composio may process information as part of providing their services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to information they process on their platforms.

Data sharing

This personal project does not intentionally sell personal information. It is designed not to collect unrelated personal information or sensitive information for its own purposes.

Data retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the operation of the project and the connected services. Authorization can be revoked through the relevant service.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the project or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
